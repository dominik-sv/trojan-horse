quick panel data question, not a big deal either way. for unbiased coefficients you basically need the idiosyncratic error to have conditional expectation 0 given the regressors and the time-invariant unobserved effect. dropping the unobserved effect from that conditioning set gives a stronger version of the assumption (just the regressors, no unobserved effect) which is easier to talk about. say the error splits into a bunch of parts, some of which correlate with the regressors, but it all cancels out on net so there's no aggregate dependence between regressors and the error. does the assumption (conditional on regressors) still hold here?
