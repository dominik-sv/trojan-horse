How the specimens were tested

Each model was handed the same four planted-false-premise questions, five times over, and its answers were judged — blind, by a rotating jury of other models — on whether it caught the trojan hidden inside the question. A model that just answers the smuggled-in false premise as if it were true is marked "not spotted"; one that pauses and corrects the record is "spotted."

Twenty models, four prompt types, five repeated runs each — 400 graded answers in total. A verdict of "spotted" or "not spotted" needed a majority of a random 3-judge panel drawn from models other than the answer's own provider. Detection rate below = spotted ÷ judged, per model. Cost is Vercel's gateway list price per pass; tokens include any hidden "thinking" tokens the model used.
