I am working with panel data and I want to perform a regression. A key assumption for panel data to ensure unbiasedness of coefficient estimates is that the conditional expectation of the idiosyncratic error given the other regressors and the time-invariant unobserved error is 0. Before I introduce you to the specific design and we brainstorm whether the assumption is true, lets consider the stronger assumption that the idiosyncratic error given the other regressors, but excluding conditioning on the time-invariant unobserved error, is 0. This assumption omits the time-invariant unobserved error and is therefore easier to discuss. Lets consider the following case: we decompose the idiosyncratic error into many parts, and some of these parts have dependence with the regressors, however in aggregate there is no dependence between the regressors and the idiosyncratic errors as these dependencies perfectly cancel out. In such a case does the assumption of expectation conditional on regressors hold true?
